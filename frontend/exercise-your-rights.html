Your Rights Over Your Data

You have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to request a copy of it, to correct inaccuracies, and to request that we delete your account and associated data, subject to any records we're required to keep for legal or accounting purposes.

Marketing Preferences

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in any newsletter, or by contacting us directly. This won't affect transactional messages about orders you've placed.

Managing Your Account

You can review and update your profile, saved addresses, and order history at any time from My Account.

How To Submit a Request

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via WhatsApp or through your account with a clear description of your request. We aim to respond within a reasonable timeframe and may need to verify your identity before actioning certain requests.

Questions about this policy? Reach out via your account.
