Privacy Notice

Last updated: September 2026

Information We Collect

When you shop with us, we collect information you provide directly — name, phone number, email, delivery address — along with order history and, if you create an account, your saved addresses and wishlist.

How We Use Your Information

We use this information to process and deliver your orders, communicate order updates, respond to support requests, and — only if you've subscribed — send occasional offers and new arrival updates.

Payment Information

We do not store your full payment card or banking credentials. Payment is handled via Cash on Delivery or through the payment method you select at checkout, with only a transaction reference retained for your order record.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. It is shared only with delivery partners to fulfil your order, and is never shared with third parties for marketing purposes without your consent.

Data Retention & Security

Order and account information is retained as long as needed to serve you and meet our legal obligations, and is protected using industry-standard safeguards appropriate to a business of our size.

Questions about this policy? Reach out via your account.
